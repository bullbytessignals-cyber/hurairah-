# How to add Hurairah's real photos

The images in this folder are **branded placeholder art**. To put his real
photos on the site, just **replace these files with his photos, keeping the
exact same filenames** — no code changes needed.

| Filename | Where it appears | Best shot to use | Ideal shape |
|---|---|---|---|
| `hero.jpg` | Homepage full-screen background | Wide/landscape shot — e.g. him with the white BMW on the road | Landscape, ≥1920px wide. Keep him on the **right half** of the frame (text sits on the left) |
| `portrait-main.jpg` | Homepage "The Trader" section | Strong portrait — e.g. the sitting pose | Portrait 4:5 |
| `story.jpg` | About page "Origins" section | Another portrait — e.g. white outfit standing shot | Portrait 4:5 |
| `desk.jpg` | About page "Beyond the charts" | Lifestyle wide shot — e.g. the sunset cars photo | Landscape 3:2 |
| `portrait-alt.jpg` | Services page "Why this mentorship" | Portrait — e.g. the cream hoodie night shot | Portrait 4:5 |
| `result-1.jpg` … `result-6.jpg` | Results gallery + homepage preview | Trade screenshots / community win posts | Portrait 4:5 (Instagram post ratio) |
| `og-cover.jpg` | Social share preview (WhatsApp/Twitter card) | Any strong landscape shot with him in it | 1200×630 |

Tips:
- JPG format, under ~500 KB each if possible (use tinypng.com to compress).
- Photos don't need to be exactly the listed ratio — the CSS crops them
  automatically (`object-fit: cover`), the ratio just tells you what part
  will be visible.
- After replacing, hard-refresh the browser (Ctrl+Shift+R) to see changes.
